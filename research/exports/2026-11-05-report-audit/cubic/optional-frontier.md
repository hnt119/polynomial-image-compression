# Optional editor note on the later T01 frontier

**Optional paragraph for the editor.** For the plus-sign cubic, a fixed saving on the full image would force almost every ordered pair to share its exact output at many scales. Place the input set in a bounded half-open interval and repeatedly divide each scalar interval into four. If an N-point set has at most N^(2−c) outputs, then, for every 0<γ<c, all but at most N^(2−γ) pairs have more than floor((c−γ)log₈N) generations at which their own output appears in two or more child squares. The generations may differ from pair to pair. Their positions, the balance between the children, and a construction realizing the saving remain open. [MC-S1 and MC-S2, equations (1)–(3)](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/c689ab52ca3bb9e3221bd09ae9a083a3111e39df/research/notes/2026-10-21-multiscale-collisions/specialist.md#L11-L121)

**Analytic proof checked in this audit.** For fixed y, the map x↦P₊(x,y) is surjective and strictly increasing because its derivative is 1+3(x−y)²≥1 and its endpoint limits are opposite infinities. Each output z therefore has a graph x=f_z(y). Writing u=f_z(y)−y gives f′_z=(3u²−1)/(3u²+1), of absolute value at most one everywhere. This includes u=0 and u=±1/√3. In each of the four half-open y-children the finitely many occupied y-values span strictly less than one child width. Their x-values span at most that distance and can meet at most two x-children. This gives eight children per exact output and parent square.

Stop when each scalar cell contains at most one input. For N≥2, a width smaller than the minimum spacing suffices. Each terminal square then represents a single ordered pair. Suppressing one-child vertices in an occupied output tree leaves a tree of degree at most eight whose leaf depths count true branches. The words for distinct leaves are prefix-free, so leaves of depth at most b have disjoint extensions to length b. There are at most 8^b such leaves per output, including the singleton case. Sum over outputs and set b=floor((c−γ)log₈N) to obtain the editor paragraph. There is no depth bound in terms of N alone.

**MC-S3, localized form.** A fixed output occupies at most min(n_J,3n_I) pairs in I×J, where n_I and n_J are scalar populations. Fixing y gives at most one x, and fixing x leaves a cubic in y of leading coefficient −1. For a block of k refinements from level j, let M(C) be the largest such capacity of a terminal descendant of each coarse cell C. Let S_C be the actual output set of C. The same prefix count gives at most 8^b low-branch descendants for each fixed C,z. Multiplying by M(C) and summing gives at most 8^b Σ_C M(C)|S_C| pairs with at most b branches in the block. Cells partition the pairs, while an output occurring in several cells contributes at each occurrence. That is the precise charge for overlap.
