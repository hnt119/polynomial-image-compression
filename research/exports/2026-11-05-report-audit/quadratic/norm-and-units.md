## Reference [11]. Norm completion and a common input set

In Appendix C.1 take a characteristic-zero field $k$ and $\Delta=b^2-4ac\ne0$. For $a\ne0$ the two roots $\alpha,\tau\alpha$ have sum $-b/a$ and product $c/a$. Thus $q=aN(x-\alpha y)$, including in the split algebra with its two factors interchanged. Writing $\beta=u+vt$ gives $2u=d$ and $bu-v\Delta=ae$. These equations yield the report's $\beta$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(\beta z)=dx+ey$. Expanding $aN(z+\tau(\beta)/a)$ recovers the additive constant $N(\beta)/a$, which the report subtracts. For $a=0$, $b\ne0$, and the product coordinates $(y,bx+cy)$ give the form directly. The displayed trace coefficient $(e-cd/b,d/b)$ then contributes exactly $dx+ey$. The correlated set of admissible $z$ is carried along in both cases. [Original checkpoint, §§2.1–2.2](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/proofs/norm-context/norm-transfer.md#L1-L72)

In §4.1, $Q(x,y)=(x-\omega y)(x-\omega^2y)$. Its identification with the field norm uses the stated hypothesis $[k(\omega):k]=2$. Expanding $Q(x+2/3,y-1/3)$ adds $x+1/3$, verifying (17). For a nonzero difference $h$ chosen as above, $B=C\cap(C-h)$ has $|B|\ge N(N-1)/|C-C|\ge (2D_0)^{-1}N^{1-\eta}$. For **every** ordered pair from $A=B/h+1/3$ the two shifted numerators lie in $C$. Thus the entire $P_0$ image lies in $h^{-2}Q(C,C)-1/3$. Substituting the input lower bound gives the stated prefactor $K_0(2D_0)^{2-c_1}$ and $2-c_1=(2-c_0)/(1-\eta)$. The restrictions $0<c_0\le1$ and $0\le\eta<c_0/2$ give $c_1>0$ and allow every strict $0<\varepsilon<c_1$ on a growing family after absorbing the prefactor. Finding simultaneous witnesses to the two hypotheses is open. This affine quadratic branch is separate from T01's fixed plus-sign cubic. The opening finite-field classification in §4.1 cites [10], Lemma 4.2. ## Reference [12]. Why a return is inside the cutoff

Source $F,K=F(s),E=K(\omega),R$ correspond to report $F,L=F(s),E=L(\omega),\mathcal R$. Throughout $d=[F:\mathbb Q]$. The cubic has one real root and remains irreducible over totally real $F$. Otherwise its unique real root would generate a subfield with a nonreal embedding. The chosen real copy of $L$ excludes $\omega$. Thus $L$ has degree $3d$ and signature $(d,d)$, and $E$ has degree $6d$ and signature $(0,3d)$. The relative order discriminants $(31)$ and $(3)$ give the reported upper bounds $D_*=31^{1/3}D_0$ and $D_E=\sqrt3 D_*$. The involution fixes $L$. The two extensions of a complex place have reciprocal moduli for an element of relative norm one. [MS-F1–F4](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-20-mixed-signature/field-units.md#L49-L261)

The carrier comprises all algebraic integers in the mixed interval/disk window. The chosen embedding into $\mathbb R$ is injective. Translating the half-window and taking differences gives $(\pi/2)^d T^{3d}/\sqrt{|\operatorname{disc}(L)|}$ as a lower bound. Norm separation gives an upper bound $(2T+1)^{3d}$. At $T_0=2\sqrt{D_*}$ the lower bound obtained using $D_*$ is $(4\pi)^d$. Multiplying $x-\omega y$ by $\alpha+\beta\omega$ reproduces the displayed matrix. Its inverse corresponds to $(\alpha-\beta)-\beta\omega$ and the two matrices have entries in $\mathcal O_L$. The determinant is the relative norm, equal to one. A return requires both target coordinates to lie in the same window at every embedding. [MS-C1](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-20-mixed-signature/carrier-rate.md#L48-L115), [MS-F5](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-20-mixed-signature/field-units.md#L329-L356)

For a nonzero returning source let $w=Q(x,y)$ and $t_i=\log|\nu_i^+(u)|$. At each complex place one of the source eigen-coordinates has modulus at most $2Te^{-|t_i|}$ because its target has modulus at most $2T$. The other has modulus at most $2T$. Their product gives $|\sigma_i w|\le4T^2e^{-|t_i|}$. At every real place $0<\sigma w\le3T^2$. The nonzero integral absolute norm of $w$ counts complex places twice, giving $\sum_i\log|\sigma_i w|\ge-(d/2)\log(3T^2)$. Together these bounds give
$$
\sum_i|t_i|\le d\log(4T^2)+(d/2)\log(3T^2)
=dH_{\rm cut}(T),\qquad H_{\rm cut}(T)=\tfrac12\log(48T^6).
$$
The factor $1/2$ appears in §4.2, and the equivalent $d/2$ appears in Appendix C.2. The height is $\|t\|_1/(3d)$, whereas the direct bound on an individual conjugate is $e^{dH_{\rm cut}(T)}$. This proof makes the finite inventory exhaustive for nonzero returns by order units. [MS-F6, (20)–(23)](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-20-mixed-signature/field-units.md#L377-L433)

## Reference [12]. Why the cutoff contains many units

The relative logarithmic lattices have dimension $d$ in the ordinary Euclidean log coordinates. Source $B$ is the report's $B_0=2\sqrt{D_E}$, and $g=\log B_0$. The product-disk argument gives an algebraic integer approximating each relative log vector within $3dg$ in the $\ell^1$ norm. The ideal count contributes $B_0^{12d}2^{6d}$ and the exact relative-norm element count contributes $(2B_0^2+1)^{3d}$. Thus $J_0=64B_0^{12}(2B_0^2+1)^3$. Equal labels here use principal **maximal-order** ideals, giving a maximal-order norm-one unit ratio. Integrating the resulting cover gives a covolume at most $(6\mathrm e gJ_0)^d=K_E^d$. Reducing units modulo $(1+2\omega)\mathcal O_E$ costs at most $3^{3d}=27^d$ in subgroup index. This cost enters once, through $K_{\mathcal R}=27K_E$. [Arithmetic proof, (11)–(28)](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-21-relative-unit-population/arithmetic.md#L118-L311)

With $T_*=\max(T_0,\exp(2K_{\mathcal R}/3))$ fixed before $j$ grows, the half-radius $\ell^1$-ball count gives at least $w_j(dH_{\rm cut}(T_*))^d/(d!K_{\mathcal R}^d)$ units. Each log vector has $w_j$ torsion lifts. Now $d!\le d^d$ and $H_{\rm cut}(T_*)\ge3\log T_*\ge2K_{\mathcal R}$ give $|U_j|\ge2^d$ at every level. MS-O1 establishes the population. The exact local return tests use the log and phase coordinates of the **same global unit** at every place. Counting centered log vectors gives no count in those source-dependent windows. [MSU-A3](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-21-relative-unit-population/arithmetic.md#L313-L350), [geometry audit](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-21-relative-unit-population/geometry-audit.md#L130-L156), [rate ledger](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-21-relative-unit-population/ledger.md#L51-L84), [source windows, (15)–(16)](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-21-typical-unit-returns/source-windows.md#L251-L289)

## Reference [13]. Which labels are occupied

Let $n=|C_j|$, $N=n^2-1$. Report $T_j(\mathbf p)$ and $o_j$ are the source return count $r(\mathbf p)$ and the number $\nu_j$ of full order-unit orbits meeting the nonzero square. The cutoff is exhaustive even though the finite inventory can fail closure under multiplication. Multiplication on a nonzero $z$ is free. Every point in a full-group orbit intersection is a target of one returning unit, and each of its $m$ members therefore has $m$ targets. Further, $-1$ pairs that point with its distinct negative. All such intersections have even size at least two. [MSR-I1, (7)–(10)](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-21-typical-unit-returns/incidences.md#L72-L132)

The joint label is the **actual principal order ideal** $z\mathcal R_j$ and the **exact field element** $z\tau_j(z)$. Equality of those ideals makes $z'/z$ invertible in the order. Equality of the norm elements makes its relative norm one. The converse follows by multiplying by such a unit. Summing reciprocal sizes over the nonzero square counts each occupied label once. Different labels can give the same output, and zero is one additional output. This proves both relations in (18). A large first moment can be dominated by a few large classes. The source's ambient ideal-divisor upper estimate also fails to certify exponential decay for the actual occupied labels. [MSR-I2–I4](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-21-typical-unit-returns/incidences.md#L133-L327), [return and rate audit](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-21-typical-unit-returns/audit.md#L69-L203)

The eventual estimate $o_j\le C(n^2-1)e^{-\gamma d_j}$ for fixed $C,\gamma>0$ is **OPEN**. If it held, (18) and $n\le e^{B_*d_j}$, $B_*=3\log(2T_*+1)$, would yield every strict $0<c<\gamma/B_*$ after absorbing the prefactor and the zero output. This is the MS-O2-I route to a full-image saving. [MS-O2 report, §§5–6](https://github.com/hnt119/polynomial-image-compression/blob/bd1f2d663262821575eebdfa559b7c25f76b46ab/research/notes/2026-10-21-typical-unit-returns/README.md#L209-L296)
