**Scaling and hybrid inputs.** The C2 parameter called $Q$ in the deformation is the report's $R$, distinct from its quadratic form $Q_\omega$. Direct substitution gives $F_R(Rx,Ry)=R^4P_4(x,y)$. For $R=1$, factoring out the first unit $g$ gives $g^4(t+2(h/g)u)^4+g(3t-(h/g)u)$. The C2 proof factors out the second unit and uses the reciprocal ratio. Both changes of variables keep the same two original units throughout the expression. The admissible pairs satisfy both membership conditions on the common unit and its ratio multiple. **The actual local ring.** The source has $v_{\mathfrak p}(7)=3$ and residue degree one. Therefore $\mathcal O/\mathfrak p^{3m}$ has $7^{3m}$ elements and the smallest positive rational integer annihilating it is $7^m$. The report's $S_m,B_m^{\rm loc}$ are the source's $R_m,E_m$. For the whole critical coset, $(x,y)\mapsto(v,x)=(x+2y,x)$ has inverse $y=(v-x)/2$ because 2 is a unit. The two independent variables give the exact support $g(3\alpha+\mathfrak p)+(7/2)\alpha+\mathfrak p^4$. As $g'(3\alpha)\in\mathfrak p^3$, the only Taylor terms below depth four are $6(3\alpha)^2u^2+4(3\alpha)u^3$. With $u=\pi b+\pi^2c$ modulo $\mathfrak p^3$, the three nonzero square digits each admit seven next digits and the zero digit admits one. Independence of $x$ fills each higher tail. This proves $N_1=4$ and $N_m=22\cdot7^{3m-4}$ for $m\ge2$. Dividing by $(1/7)^2$ gives $4/7$ and $22/49$ after normalizing by ring size. **Carrier and exact width.** There are $d/3$ cubic completions and $N(\mathfrak I)=7^{d/3}$. The half-cube translation average and subtraction of one point put at least $(X/(7^{1/3}\sqrt D))^d$ elements into the centered ideal window. Translation by $-1$ selects the critical residue 6. At $X=2\,7^{1/3}\sqrt D$, this lower bound is $2^d$. The same $X$ gives $I=[-1-X,-1+X]$ and $X>7/2$. On $I^2$ convexity gives the maximum at a vertex. Substitution into the four vertices gives $U=81(X+1)^4-2X-2$. For the minimum, fix $v=(x+1)+2(y+1)$ and minimize the linear term along the feasible line segment. On the first branch $w=v-3$ minimizes $w^4-w/2-7X/2-7/2$ at $w=1/2$, feasible for $X\ge7/2$. The second branch is increasing from their common endpoint. Thus the minimum is $-7X/2-59/16$ and their difference is exactly $81(X+1)^4+3X/2+27/16$. This derivation applies to the continuous square. **Sign of the certificate.** CRT permits at most $N_m^{d/3}$ classes. Within each class division of a nonzero difference by $7^m$ gives a nonzero algebraic integer, so the sup norm separation is at least $7^m$. The resulting envelope is $N_m^{d/3}(1+W/7^m)^d$. Subtracting its log from twice the input log lower bound gives (5.5). On the diagonal $P_4(t,t)=81t^4+2t$. The midpoint difference is $81X^4+486b^2X^2$ at $b=-1$, so $(W+7^m)/X^2\ge18\,7^{m/2}$. Together with $D\ge7^{2/3}$ this makes $\kappa_m<0$. C4 §6 separately charges the saturation, mixed-depth and full-support ideal covers. Branch-dependent ranges, smaller realized support and the actual image remain outside the retired certificate.
