## 3. Scope checklist

- **Parameter order:** choose the C0 tower and \(D_0\), then a fixed radius,
  then let \(j\) grow. The variable-radius seed obstruction explicitly assumes
  \(T_j\ge T_0\).
- **Same set and projection:** both coordinates use all of \(A_j\);
  \(\sigma_0\) is injective. No non-Cartesian engineered subset replaces
  \(A_j^2\).
- **Global actions:** each action is one actual unit of
  \(\mathcal O_{K_j}[\omega]\), not separate rotations at different places.
- **Field involution:** the relative involution differs from ordinary
  complex conjugation at nonreal \(K_j\)-places.
- **Integer norm:** \(Q(x,y)\ne0\) at a nonzero pair because the chosen real
  embedding is positive definite; all complex places are counted twice.
- **Stabilizers:** distinct units give distinct targets at nonzero pairs;
  zero has one actual target.
- **Order and denominators:** selected actions have integral coefficients,
  but maximal-order passage has its charged index. Class-number estimates
  and favorable splitting assumptions are unused.
- **Regulator and unit heights:** relative log normalization is explicit.
  Its absolute growth and useful basis shape are unknown here.
- **Overlap and exceptions:** continuous upper bounds do not become a
  discrete lower-tail theorem. Fixed-field \(o_K(1)\) is not uniform in
  the tower.
- **Rate:** no \(\varepsilon,\delta,c>0\) is proved. The support implication
  is proved with all outputs and constants accounted for.
- **Failure boundary:** MS-C3 closes only the seed-and-torsion action
  certificate. Neither the lattice carrier nor the unrestricted target
  is disproved.
- **Historical boundaries:** BFF-O1 stays retired; the totally real norm
  sieve certificates stay closed; RC15/4 remains OPEN.
