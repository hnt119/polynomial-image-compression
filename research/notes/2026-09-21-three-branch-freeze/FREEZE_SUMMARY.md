21 September 2026. Documentation only; mathematical state from 16 September.

  six-point gadget has $n_m=4\cdot2^m+2$ and
  $s_m=n_m^2-O(n_m^{3/2})$. Its rates satisfy $\rho=4=\lambda^2$.
- **Strongest failed continuation:** that entire literal family fails
  despite unbounded input growth. Sufficiently separated copies also fail,

- **Structural result:** the explicit quadratic pair map $T_\tau$
  preserves every output and obeys
  $T_\sigma\circ T_\tau=T_{\sigma+\tau}$. It is a map of pairs,
  not independent scalar branches.
- **Current bottleneck:** quantitative compatibility of that motion with
  a common finite Cartesian square. Full finite-square invariance is
  impossible for $\tau\ne0$; useful partial returns remain OPEN.

  decide whether $\limsup_{N\to\infty}R_1(N)>0$ or prove
  $R_1(N)\to0$. This obligation is frozen, not begun.
