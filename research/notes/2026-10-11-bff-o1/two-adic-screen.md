# The second approach: a stopped 2-adic reconstruction screen

11 October 2026 · BFF-O1 · lead · version 1.
Status: **OPEN as a reconstruction argument; stopped when the tower proof
supplied the decisive all-target result.** This records the second and last
structured approach tested. No mathematical program, finite scout, carrier
mutation, action enlargement, or extra classification was performed.

For \(b=h\xi^a\prod_{\ell<r}(2-\xi^{i_\ell7^\ell})\), put
\(d_\ell=i_\ell7^\ell\) and \(e=a+\sum d_\ell\pmod q\).
The exact identity
\[
b=(-1)^r h\xi^e\prod_{\ell<r}(1-2\xi^{-d_\ell})
\]
makes reduction modulo powers of 2 a possible way to recover the words
without expanding their exponentially large full supports.

For two odd scales the complete key modulo 2 is
\[
\mathcal K(v,w)\equiv
\xi^{-2e}+\xi^{-2f}+\xi^{e-f}+\xi^{-e+f}+\xi^{-e-f}
\pmod {2\mathbb Z[\xi]}.
\]
The leading residues of a target in \(B\cup(-B)\) are either zero or
a single phase, while those of a target in \(D\) are zero or
\(\xi^a+\xi^{-a}\). Thus at this precision the target key has at
most eight monomial contributions, despite the growing original support.
For odd \(h\), the next useful square signature is
\[
\frac{b^2}{h^2\xi^{2e}}
\equiv1+4\sum_{\ell<r}
 (\xi^{-2d_\ell}-\xi^{-d_\ell})\pmod 8.
\]
These are exact algebraic expansions, not full-fiber estimates.

The intended reconstruction would first separate phase centers modulo
\(q/7\), and then recover the word from this square signature. The
seven-slot cyclotomic relation must still be applied at each precision;
coefficients cannot be treated as formally independent. The independent
auditor inspected the proposed word-signature argument, but a complete
all-target reconstruction and population theorem from these congruences
was not adopted or required.

In particular the simplest equal-2-adic-valuation scale restriction has
only \(4^2+2^2+1^2+1^2=22\) ordered scale pairs, hence \(22q^4\)
sources before any further exclusions. This is below the requested
\(32q^4\) threshold. It cannot by itself prove the exact BFF-O1
statement. No extension to mixed valuations is asserted here.

The accepted proof uses the degree-seven last layer, finite-dimensional
polynomial maps, and uniform polynomial-zero-set bounds instead. This
congruence approach is neither a refutation of the carrier nor a new open
research commission. It is not a dependency of the final theorem.
