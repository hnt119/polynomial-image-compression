## Budget and exact execution

and 10,000 compatibility/accounting checks. They were not enlarged.

actual-image stopping argument were derived before enumeration. The lead

Every scalar is represented as h times a rational polynomial in the fixed
cubic root t. Since h>0 and the cubic is irreducible, reduced rational
coefficient triples determine equality exactly. The real embedding is
specified by 0<t<1/12; no numerical closeness is used. The exact difference-
cube and expanded-cube evaluators are checked against each other on every
actual square cell. This uses one fixed number field, not a growing tower.

The three final squares have 16+36+64=116 cells, so the two evaluators
use 232 charged P evaluations. Actual accounting checks, unit-test costs,
runtime, input/code/test/proof hashes and raw-output identities are retained
in exact-audit.md and the ignored run folder. Existing fixed repository
