## Invariant polynomial libraries

The ambient carrier A is arbitrary, with its actual N. Each rule is
defined everywhere on the same finite E and maps E into E. Injectivity
then gives a permutation; this is a substantive hypothesis.

NC-A2 permits arbitrary rule count and degree, with classes at most two.
There is no projection loss: u is an injective coordinate on a fixed fiber.
One output per generated class costs at least Q/2; the complement adds
N²−Q. The resulting certificate is at least N²/2 for every Q.

NC-A5 permits polynomial output relabeling but forces an injective affine
output map. Orbits have at most four pairs and at most two outputs.
The correct charge is each orbit's own number of outputs, at least half
its pair count. Neither argument proves different orbits have different
outputs. **Actual image lower bounds are not asserted.**

No thinning, field arithmetic, moduli, regulator or digit carries occur.
Uniformity in arbitrary real coefficients is proved by order and polynomial
invertibility; it does not supply a successful construction whose heights
could be ignored. Partial-return graphs are excluded explicitly.
