### G.4 A genuine change to the number-field mechanism

This subsidiary test changed the total-reality assumption rather than retrying a frozen CM-unit construction.

Let \(s\) be the real root of \(s^3+s+1=0\), put \(L=\mathbb Q(s)\), and let
 \[
 u=s^2+s\omega.
 \]
The polynomial is irreducible by the rational-root test, and its positive derivative gives signature \((1,1)\). The identity
 \[
 (s-1)(s^3+s+1)=s^4-s^3+s^2-1
 \]
proves \(N_{L(\omega)/L}(u)=1\). Both \(u\) and \(u^{-1}=\bar u\) are integral, so this action has no \(S\)-unit denominator loss.

It is non-torsion. If \(u\) were a root of unity, \(u+u^{-1}=2s^2-s\) would be totally real. This element is irrational, since otherwise \(s\) would satisfy a quadratic equation over \(\mathbb Q\). It therefore generates the cubic field \(L\), contradicting its nonreal embeddings.

This is an explicit algebraic gain, but no suitably controlled finite Cartesian coefficient set was constructed from it. It contributes no proved improvement to (A.1).
