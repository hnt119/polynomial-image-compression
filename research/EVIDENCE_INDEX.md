## Rational corollary

The public [November Theorem 1](proofs/c0-v5/theorem-and-proof.tex) covers every fixed integral square-plus-linear quadruple. The 5 November academic edition, §3.6, printed p. 13, derives a separate corollary for every fixed rational-coefficient quadratic $f$ whose homogeneous quadratic part has rank one. Its normalization is recorded here from the final editable proof. Clear denominators so that $mf=\alpha x^2+\beta xy+\gamma y^2+\delta x+\eta y+\kappa$ has integer coefficients with $m>0$. Rank one gives $\beta^2=4\alpha\gamma$. If $\alpha\ne0$, then

$$
4\alpha(mf-\kappa)=(2\alpha x+\beta y)^2+4\alpha\delta x+4\alpha\eta y.
$$

The right side has Theorem 1's form, and the affine map on outputs has nonzero slope. If $\alpha=0$, then $\beta=0$ and $\gamma\ne0$; interchange the two variables, which permutes $A^2$. The sets and exponent for the normalized polynomial therefore work for $f$. The proof of Theorem 1 and its external field dependency carry through. This mathematical derivative records the scope added in the later academic edition. The archived September and November theorem texts retain their original statements.

## T01: from scalar sharing to exact branching

Writing \(u=x-y\) and \(z=P(x,y)\) gives the two actual endpoints \((z-u^3\pm u)/2\). Prescribing a fiber or a compatible motif gives a set \(E\subseteq A^2\). Write \(N=|A|,\ Q=|E|,\ Z=|P(E)|\). The safe certificate is \(Z+N^2-Q\): every omitted pair can add an output. A lower bound on this **certificate** is not a lower bound on the **actual image**. Likewise, a small number of output intervals at one resolution can hide many distinct exact values.

In MC-S2, enclose the finite set in a bounded half-open root interval; quarter intervals until each terminal scalar cell contains at most one input. If \(b_L(p)\) counts genuine branching generations for the exact output of the pair \(p\), then
\[
 \#\{p\in A^2:b_L(p)\le b\}\le |P(A,A)|\,8^b\qquad (b\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}).
\]
For each \(0<\gamma<c\), if \(|P(A,A)|\le N^{2-c}\), all but at most \(N^{2-\gamma}\) pairs have more than \(\lfloor(c-\gamma)\log_8 N\rfloor\) such generations. This counts exact equalities. The [six-case census](notes/2026-10-21-multiscale-collisions/experiment-summary.json) and [independent counts](notes/2026-10-21-multiscale-collisions/referee-counts.json) are finite evidence on 533 ordered pairs. ## T03: two distinctions that survive the later cycles

The [T03 reconciliation](tracks/03-eisenstein-cartesianization/STATUS.md) records the finite optimizer counts and the missing RC15/4 premise. For the different [mixed-signature carrier](notes/2026-10-20-mixed-signature/README.md), [MSU-A1–A3](notes/2026-10-21-relative-unit-population/arithmetic.md) resolve the population question MS-O1 affirmatively at a fixed radius \(T_*\). Membership in that inventory is not membership in a translated source window. The [return-window proof](notes/2026-10-21-typical-unit-returns/source-windows.md) retains the missing phases. For nonzero input pairs, the [orbit-incidence proof](notes/2026-10-21-typical-unit-returns/incidences.md) gives \(N_j\mathbb E(1/R_j)=\nu_j\), where \(N_j\) counts those pairs, \(R_j\) is their genuine return count and \(\nu_j\) counts occupied arithmetic labels. The full quadratic support is at most \(1+\nu_j\). An exponentially improved bound on those **occupied** labels remains the [MS-O2-I obligation](notes/2026-10-21-typical-unit-returns/README.md); it was not established by the failed ambient count.
