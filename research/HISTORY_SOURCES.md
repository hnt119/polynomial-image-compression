# History source recovery

On 5 September 2026, 33 unique research attachments were recovered from earlier
project tasks and copied unchanged into `history-sources/`. Two filenames were
attached in more than one task; their contents matched byte-for-byte and one
canonical copy is retained. The manifest records every source task where each
attachment was encountered, including inherited attachments in branched tasks.
Attachment presence alone does not assign a result to that task's current track.

The four-track selection is recorded in [PROJECT_HISTORY.md](PROJECT_HISTORY.md).
The separate “Unit Distance and Sum-Product” task supplies shared Phase A/B and
original C0 background. It is not counted as a fifth direction.

## Inspection and proof status

The history uses the named statement and conclusion sections of the recent cubic
checkpoints, the CR verdict and composition report, and the existing C1 review/C2
prompt. Source theorem labels are preserved as attributed results. Background
reports are archived for traceability, not independently certified by this import.
PDF text was extracted for inventory and relevant statement review; this was not
a new visual or mathematical audit of every page. Historical C++ programs were
inspected as source files and archived, but not executed or added to the current
experiment suite.

No conversation transcript, email draft, private account path, or administrator
file is included. The recovered text was checked for obvious personal paths,
contact addresses, and credential strings. This is a bounded content check.
