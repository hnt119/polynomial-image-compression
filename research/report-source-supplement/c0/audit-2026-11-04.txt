MP-02. Nonspeciality, printed p.9 / PDF p.11.
Under ab(ae-bc) nonzero, additive special form is impossible: a linear outer polynomial has no xy term; otherwise degree two forces quadratic outer and affine inners, so the linear term must be parallel to ax+by. This contradicts ae-bc nonzero. Multiplicative form forces outer degree one and both inner degrees one, so its homogeneous degree-two part is a nonzero multiple of xy, which cannot match a^2 x^2+2abxy+b^2 y^2 when ab nonzero. The degree factorizations are exhaustive over R. Degenerate choices can be special while still compressible. Matrix rank of the quadratic part is a different issue from the determinant ae-bc.
Status: PROVED, independently checked. No stronger classification claimed.

MP-03. Auxiliary deformation, printed pp.8,10 / PDF pp.10,12.

MP-05. Actual input counts, Section 3.3, printed p.11 / PDF p.13.
B_K(X) consists of algebraic integers with every real conjugate bounded by X. Covolume is sqrt(|Delta_K|)=D^(d/2). Averaging translates of a half-open cube of side X yields at least X^d/D^(d/2) lattice points in one translate. Subtracting one point maps all of them injectively into the centered box of radius X, including the zero difference. Thus n >= (X/sqrt D)^d. A nonzero algebraic integer has nonzero integral norm, so at least one conjugate has modulus >=1. Distinct embedded lattice points are sup-norm separated by >=1, giving n <= (2X+1)^d by disjoint interiors of side-one cubes. These are uniform in degree and need no equidistribution, regulator estimate, minimum Euclidean separation assumption, or successive-minima bound.
Status: PROVED, independently checked. Lower count proves unbounded actual cardinality because X/sqrt D>1.

MP-06. Full output count, Section 3.4, printed pp.11-12 / PDF pp.13-14.
Complete splitting and CRT give O_K/R O_K isomorphic to product_i F_(p_i)^d. The allowed square containers therefore have product cardinality (rho R)^d. This does not require B_K(X) to fill any residue class. For every ordered alpha,beta in B_K(X), the all-conjugate output bound H=H_P X^2 follows from R<X. Within one residue class, two distinct outputs differ by R gamma for integral nonzero gamma, so sup-norm separation >=R. Side-R cubes yield at most (2H/R+1)^d elements in each class, uniformly. Thus the full image is at most [rho(2H+R)]^d <= (C_P rho X^2)^d <= (C_P rho D)^d n^2=theta^d n^2. Input thinning, parameter fibers, exceptional pairs and output branches introduce no additional cost: there is no thinning or parameterization, all pairs lie in the same container, and no exceptions are discarded. Denominator R, coefficient height C_P and discriminant D are explicitly charged. Field degrees vary but enter only as the amplification exponent. No regulator or unit population is used in this construction.
Status: PROVED, independently checked. H=0 yields per-class bound one, so the zero polynomial is covered without a division by zero.

MP-07. Real transport and exponent, Section 3.5, printed pp.12-13 / PDF pp.14-15.
A_j=R^-1 sigma_j(B_Fj(X)); field embedding and nonzero scalar maps are injective. Evaluation commutes with sigma_j since coefficients are integral. Hence input and output cardinalities are unchanged under transport. With epsilon_P=-log theta/log(2X+1)>0 and n_j <= (2X+1)^d_j, we get n_j^epsilon_P <= theta^-d_j, or theta^d_j <= n_j^-epsilon_P. The lower input bound is used for growth, not this final exponent conversion.
