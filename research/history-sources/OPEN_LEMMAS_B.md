# OPEN_LEMMAS_B

## O1. Sparse local value-set criterion

- **Statement sought:** Give checkable algebraic conditions on a fixed bivariate polynomial \(F\) ensuring that
  \[
  |F(\mathbb F_p,\mathbb F_p)|\le q_p p
  \]
  with \(q_p\le1-\eta\) for a useful collection of primes and a fixed \(\eta>0\).
- **Why needed:** it would turn local candidate selection into a theorem rather than an ad hoc computation.
- **Status:** OPEN.

## O2. Removable arithmetic deformation

- **Statement sought:** Characterize families \(F_Q\in\mathbb Z[x,y]\) for which many primes dividing \(Q\) force sparse reduced images and for which there are affine bijections \(\tau_Q,\eta_Q\) satisfying
  \[
  F_Q(\tau_Q(x),\tau_Q(y))=\eta_Q(F(x,y))
  \]
  for one fixed polynomial \(F\).
- **Why needed:** this is the bridge between engineered congruences and a fixed-polynomial theorem.
- **Status:** OPEN beyond the verified quadratic example.

## O3. Higher-degree local-density threshold

- **Statement sought:** For degree \(m>2\), construct a fixed-polynomial family for which
  \[
  \rho D\left(2H X^{m-2}+R/X^2\right)<1
  \]
  while \(R\), \(H\), and \(X\) satisfy the required integrality and rescaling constraints.
- **Why needed:** this would show that local compression is not essentially quadratic.
- **Status:** OPEN.

## O4. Prescribed split-prime compatibility

- **Statement sought:** Determine which finite prescribed sets of rational primes can be made to split completely throughout a bounded-root-discriminant totally real tower suitable for the carrier construction.
- **Why needed:** a candidate polynomial may be sparse only at specific primes, as \(xy(x+y)\) is at \(p=7\).
- **Status:** not supplied by Pohoata's Proposition 3.1 in the required prescribed-prime form.

## O5. Prime-power amplification

- **Statement sought:** Bound the image density of a polynomial modulo \(\mathfrak p^k\) and combine it with residue-class packing modulo \(p^k\mathcal O_K\).
- **Why needed:** it could amplify a restriction available at only one rational prime.
- **Status:** OPEN; ramification and lifting multiplicities must be audited.

## O6. Rank-two correlated-sum lemma

- **Statement sought:** Give hypotheses under which
  \[
  \{U_1(g)V_1(p)+U_2(g)V_2(p)\}
  \]
  has exponentially smaller support than the full parameter space.
- **Why needed:** local-to-global compression does not help polynomials whose reduced images are full.
- **Status:** OPEN from Cycle 1.

## O7. Local-to-global lemma beyond boxes

- **Statement sought:** Replace the symmetric Minkowski box by a more general carrier while retaining a uniform per-residue-class bound with a computable exponent \(u\).
- **Why needed:** carrier geometry may need to match the degree or monomial structure of future polynomials.
- **Status:** OPEN.
