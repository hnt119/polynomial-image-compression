# Shared result: squares of linear forms plus linear terms

**Source scope.** This is a mathematical digest of Theorem 1, §§3.1–3.5 (printed pp. 9–13), in the 3 November 2026 revision and its 4 November proof audit, MP-01–MP-07. The personal report and administrative companions stay local. This edition inspected the statement, counting ledger and audit locators; it did not repeat the proof audit or independently prove the field-supply theorem. This is a proof **sketch**, not a substitute for the cited external arithmetic input.

**MP-01 — PROVED in the source theorem system.** For each fixed integer quadruple \((a,b,c,e)\), let \(L=ax+by\), \(M=cx+ey\) and \(P=L^2+M\). There are a fixed \(\epsilon_P>0\) and arbitrarily large finite \(A_j\subset\mathbb R\) with \(|P(A_j,A_j)|\le |A_j|^{2-\epsilon_P}\). In particular no primitivity or nonvanishing hypothesis is needed for this **compression** statement. The narrower corrected C0 statement in the earlier sources is preserved. In MP-02 the additional condition \(ab(ae-bc)\ne0\) identifies a nonspecial subfamily; it is not a construction cost. Rank zero (including \(P=0\)) is allowed. No full-rank quadratic or cubic target follows.

**MP-03–04 — exact deformation and external input.** The proof chooses squarefree \(R\) and uses \(P_R=L^2+RM\), so \(P_R(Rx,Ry)=R^2P(x,y)\). Proposition 3.8 of *Planar Point Sets with Many Unit Distances* is the source's EXTERNAL RESULT. For sufficiently large \(\ell\) it supplies \(t=\lfloor(\ell-1)^2/100\rfloor\) split primes \(p_i\equiv1\pmod4\) and totally real layers of unbounded degree \(d\), with common root discriminant \(D\) and \(\log D=O(\ell\log\ell)\). The tower and primes come together. Put \(R=\prod_i p_i\), \(\rho=\prod_i(p_i+1)/(2p_i)\), and \(C_P=2[(|a|+|b|)^2+|c|+|e|]+1\). Each factor in \(\rho\) is at most \(3/5\); fix \(\ell\) large enough for \(\theta=C_PD\rho<1\), then fix \(X>\max(R,\sqrt D)\) before the degree grows.

**MP-05–07 — all-pair count and real transport.** In a layer \(K\), take the algebraic integers with all real conjugates in \([-X,X]\), forming \(B\) with \(n=|B|\). Translating a half-open cube and taking differences gives \((X/\sqrt D)^d\le n\); separation by the nonzero integral norm gives \(n\le(2X+1)^d\). CRT and the square restriction modulo the split primes leave at most \((R\rho)^d\) residue classes for all outputs. Outputs in one class are separated by at least \(R\) in maximum norm, in a cube of radius \(H=(C_P-1)X^2/2\). Consequently
\[
|P_R(B,B)|\le [\rho(2H+R)]^d\le(C_P\rho X^2)^d\le\theta^d n^2.
\]
There is no exception set. The same real set \(A=R^{-1}\sigma(B)\) serves both input positions under a field embedding \(\sigma\). The upper input bound yields the endpoint \(\epsilon_P=-\log\theta/\log(2X+1)>0\), and the lower bound gives unbounded cardinality. The 4 November audit checked these internal implications and parameter order, including degenerate coefficients. The source's upstream arithmetic dependencies were not reproved and no novelty assessment was made.
